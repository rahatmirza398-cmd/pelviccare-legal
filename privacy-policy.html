PelvicCare Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Local-First Wellness & Optional Private Cloud Sync

1. Overview

PelvicCare is a local-first pelvic floor exercise and bladder diary companion app. All core features work offline on your device without creating an account. You may optionally sign in with Google to synchronize your records across devices using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore).

2. Information Collected & Stored

  • Health & Wellness Records (User-Entered): Guided exercise sessions (timestamps, duration, repetitions, hold/relax timing, plan level) and bladder diary logs (urination/fluid intake amounts, beverage types, urgency levels 0–4, leakage episodes and severity, nighttime urination flags, and optional personal notes).
  • Reminder Schedules & Preferences: Reminder times, repeat weekdays, optional display name, active plan, custom exercise settings, fluid units (ml/fl oz), audio cue settings, and language choice.
  • Optional Google Account Data: Collected only if you tap Sign in with Google — Google account email address, display name, and Firebase user identifier (UID).

3. Storage, Security & Backup Protections

  • Offline by Default: When you do not sign in, all records stay strictly in your device's private app storage.
  • Android Backup Exclusion: Sensitive local databases and preference files are excluded from Android Auto Backup and device-to-device backups.
  • Encrypted Cloud Sync (Optional): When signed in with Google, records are transmitted over HTTPS/TLS to Google Cloud Firestore and protected by strict per-user owner-only security rules.
  • No Ads, No Tracking SDKs: PelvicCare contains zero advertisements, no advertising ID collection, and no third-party analytics SDKs.
  • PII-Free Diagnostics: Production diagnostic logs exclude email addresses, display names, photo URLs, and raw user IDs.

4. Sharing & Exports

We never sell or share your personal or health data with advertisers or data brokers. Health summary reports (.txt / .csv) are generated locally on your device and leave the device only when you explicitly share them through Android's system Share sheet.

5. Deleting Your Data & Account

  • Clear All Data (In-App): Permanently deletes all exercise sessions, bladder diary entries, reminders, and scheduled alarms/notifications locally and in Cloud Firestore (when signed in), while keeping your optional Google Sign-In link.
  • Delete Account (In-App): Permanently deletes all Firestore sessions, diary entries, reminders, your Firestore profile, your Firebase Authentication account, and all local data, preferences, and scheduled notifications.
  • External Deletion Without Reinstalling: See our Account & Cloud Data Deletion page or email pelviccare.support@gmail.com with subject PelvicCare Account Deletion Request from your signed-in Google email address.

6. Contact & Technical Support

For privacy questions, external account deletion requests, or technical support, please contact our official developer support email: pelviccare.support@gmail.com.
