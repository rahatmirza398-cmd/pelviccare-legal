PelvicCare — Account & Cloud Data Deletion

Permanent deletion instructions for Google Play users (in-app and without reinstalling the app).

Local-First Privacy

1. Who Needs Cloud Account Deletion?

PelvicCare works offline by default without an account or registration. If you used PelvicCare without signing in with Google, no user account or cloud health records exist on our servers—uninstalling the app or using Clear All Data inside the app erases all local records on your device.

If you opted into Google Sign-In, your optional cloud backup is stored under your Firebase Authentication account in Google Cloud Firestore. You can permanently delete that account and all associated cloud data using either method below.

2. Request Account Deletion Without Reinstalling the App (Web / Email)

If you have already uninstalled PelvicCare or no longer have access to your device, follow these steps to request complete deletion of your PelvicCare account and cloud records:

  1. Send an email from the exact Google account email address you used to sign in to PelvicCare.
  2. Address the email to: pelviccare.support@gmail.com.
  3. Use the subject line: PelvicCare Account Deletion Request.
  4. We verify account ownership from your registered Google email address (we may reply once to confirm if needed).
  5. Once verified, all cloud records and your authentication account are permanently deleted within 7 calendar days, and you receive confirmation by email.
Email Request Template
To: pelviccare.support@gmail.com Subject: PelvicCare Account Deletion Request Hello PelvicCare Support, Please permanently delete my PelvicCare Firebase Authentication account and all associated Cloud Firestore data (profile, exercise sessions, bladder diary entries, and reminders). Google Sign-In email address: [enter your registered Google email]
Open Pre-Filled Deletion Email

3. Delete Immediately Inside the PelvicCare App

  1. Open PelvicCare and tap the Profile & Settings tab.
  2. In the Google Cloud Sync card, tap Delete Account.
  3. Review the summary and confirm Delete Permanently (you may be asked to verify with your Google account).

To erase only health records & reminders while keeping your Google Sign-In link active, use Data, Privacy & Legal → Clear All Data instead.

4. Data Deleted vs. Data Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Immediately (or Within 7 Days for External Requests):
    • Firebase Authentication user record (UID, email, display name, credential link)
    • Firestore user profile (/users/{uid})
    • All Firestore exercise sessions (/users/{uid}/sessions)
    • All Firestore bladder diary entries (/users/{uid}/diary_entries)
    • All Firestore reminders (/users/{uid}/reminders)
    • When deleted in-app: all local SQLite/Room records, app preferences, and scheduled reminder alarms/notifications
  • Retained Data: Zero personal or health records are retained after account deletion is completed. Standard deletion confirmation emails (if you contacted support) contain only the correspondence thread required to confirm completion and do not retain health logs.
